🎵🚀 Excited to unveil my latest project—a music streaming platform inspired by Spotify! 🚀🎵

Tech Stack:
HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB, Passport.js, Multer

Key Features:
✅ Custom Playlists: Users can create and delete playlists, curating their perfect music collection.

✅ Song Management: Add any track to your personalized playlists—your music, your way!

✅ Like & Unlike Songs: Keep your favorite tracks in the 'Liked Songs' playlist or remove them anytime.

✅ Quick Search: Easily find any song with a seamless search experience.

🔐 Secure Authentication: Hassle-free signup and login with Passport.js.

🔒 Admin Panel: A standout feature! Admins can upload songs, enriching the music library. 🚀🎶
